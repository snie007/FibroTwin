VESPA CHD USA dossier

This page summarizes the current source-constrained draft of the VESPA dossier on congenital heart disease in the United States. Every retained figure comes from the original approved PubMed result sets captured in the VESPA search log.

Why this draft is conservative

Codex did successfully run in a temporary worktree and found several useful-looking extra papers, but those PMIDs were outside the approved search-result sets. They were intentionally excluded from the final merge so the dossier stays provenance-clean.

Key caveat

The approved PMID set is strong for prevalence, mortality rate, surgery volume, and operative outcomes, but weaker for a single modern national long-term survival estimate by lesion class. The final draft therefore uses transparent operative-outcome and survivor-count proxies instead of overstating certainty.
