VESPA literature library

VESPA is a grant-search evidence repository organised by project. It stores search logs, PMID-linked evidence entries, and output-ready reports for grant applications.

Active dossier: USA congenital heart disease statistics

First VESPA dossier focused on congenital heart disease in the United States, covering prevalence, mortality, survival, surgery counts, and surgery types.

  • Search log: searches/VESPA/chd_usa_2026/search_log.md
  • Evidence schema: searches/VESPA/chd_usa_2026/evidence_template.yaml
  • Extracted evidence: searches/VESPA/chd_usa_2026/evidence_entries.yaml
  • Candidate source triage: searches/VESPA/chd_usa_2026/candidate_sources.md
  • Overleaf starter: reports/overleaf/main.tex

Repository structure

Raw exports live in data/raw, cleaned evidence records in data/processed, dossier-specific searches in searches/, and publishable outputs in reports/ and site/.
