\section{Purpose}
This dossier compiles the latest PMID-backed statistics for congenital heart disease (CHD) in the United States for use in the VESPA grant application. The goal is to provide traceable evidence on case numbers, mortality, survival, surgery volume, and major surgery classes.

\section{Questions to answer}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item How many people in the United States are living with congenital heart disease?
  \item How many new cases occur each year?
  \item How many deaths are attributed to CHD, and in which age groups?
  \item What are the reported survival rates?
  \item How many cardiac surgeries or interventions are performed, and which procedure classes dominate?
\end{itemize}
