# Specific Aims reference map, ventricular arrhythmias R01

This file maps candidate citation points in the Specific Aims page to explicit PubMed IDs. The aim is not to overload the aims page with references, but to ensure that every important factual claim can be backed by a real source and later expanded into a full R01 reference set.

## Claim-to-reference map

| Claim or sentence fragment | Suggested PMID(s) | Why it fits |
|---|---:|---|
| Ventricular arrhythmias are an important cause of morbidity and mortality / sudden cardiac death | 29097296, 31075787 | Major US guideline and VT ablation consensus both frame ventricular arrhythmias as important causes of morbidity and mortality. |
| Catheter ablation is a central clinical decision point in VT management | 31075787 | Expert consensus focused on catheter ablation of ventricular arrhythmias. |
| Cardiac physiologic pacing, including CRT and conduction system pacing, is used to avoid or mitigate heart failure | 37283271 | 2023 HRS/APHRS/LAHRS guideline explicitly frames CRT/CSP as pacing strategies to mitigate or prevent HF. |

## High-priority references for the aims page itself

If the aims page stays lightly referenced, these are the strongest first citations to keep on-page:

1. **PMID 29097296** — 2017 AHA/ACC/HRS guideline on ventricular arrhythmias and sudden cardiac death.
2. **PMID 31075787** — 2019 expert consensus on catheter ablation of ventricular arrhythmias.
3. **PMID 37283271** — 2023 guideline on cardiac physiologic pacing / CRT / conduction system pacing.
4. **PMID 34777827** — ventricular ECGi review including limitations for ablation localization.
5. **PMID 15649241** — inverse imaging is highly ill-posed.
6. **PMID 31993492** or **33421051** — Brugada pathophysiology and conduction/substrate mechanisms.
7. **PMID 33303478** — translational applications of computational modelling for arrhythmias.
8. **PMID 32448065** — calibration, uncertainty, and discrepancy in mechanistic electrophysiology models.
