# Independent review notes for `ffecg_business_case.md`

## Verdict
The initial draft needed edits before being considered safe.

## Main issues found
- A few opening and value-proposition sentences were stronger than the cited literature directly supported.
- Some phrases implied comparative advantage over ECGi or other workflows without direct source support.
- Some modelling claims needed softer wording such as "may" or "have the potential to".
- The CMR citations supported pre-procedure substrate definition, but not a broad claim that imaging is only an adjunct and not a substitute.

## Changes applied after review
- Softened the short-answer section to avoid unsupported comparative claims.
- Reframed ffECG as an additional decision-support layer rather than a demonstrated middle-ground solution.
- Changed stronger business-language claims to cautious plausibility statements.
- Softened modelling language from established benefit to potential/supportive value.
- Tightened the bottom-line business case so it stays explicitly hypothetical pending prospective validation.

## Current standard to maintain
The document should continue to make only these classes of claims:
1. high-burden clinical need,
2. real limitations in current VT / CRT workflows,
3. ECGi inverse-problem limitations,
4. computational modelling as a promising but not yet outcome-proven direction,
5. ffECG as a candidate decision-support tool that still requires prospective validation.
