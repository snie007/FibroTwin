For organisers

This page is a polished starter draft. Before using it live, swap any clue text you want, confirm your event route, and make sure every stop stays within approved, supervised areas.

  • Keep the trail to event-approved spaces only.
  • Avoid boarding houses, private routes, or precise wayfinding.
  • Use staff checkpoints if you want prize codes or stamp cards.
  • Add team names, QR clues, or sponsor messages later if needed.

Nice extras you could add later

  • A secret word at each stop to unlock a final riddle
  • House or team scoring for friendly competition
  • A timed challenge for the fastest supervised team
  • A photo prompt at the final stop

If you want, this can also be turned into a more customised event microsite with your real clues, colours, and prize messaging.
