Two structured literature reviews were performed. Review A targeted seasonality in cardiac size, shape, and function, emphasizing human studies and imaging-derived phenotypes. Review B targeted classical and modern statistical methods for detecting seasonality, with emphasis on methods transferable to irregularly timed biomedical observations.

For Review A, four PubMed query families were used, spanning direct cardiac imaging studies, ventricular function studies, related cardiovascular physiology, and heart-failure seasonality. For Review B, four query families were used, spanning cosinor and harmonic regression, irregular-sampling methods, generalized additive models with cyclic smooths, and circular seasonality tests. Top-ranked records were exported, deduplicated within the exported set, and screened manually.
